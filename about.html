😈 ABOUT Z PAJAL

Har kadam par troll aur dhokha... Gate tak pahunchna asaan nahi!

🚪 1. The Exit Door Troll

🤪 Z PAJAL ka ek hi goal hai: Apne character ko aakhiri Exit Door (Gate) tak le jana. Lekin jaise hi aap gate ke paas pahunchenge, darwaza achanak bhaag jayega, gayab ho jayega ya rasta hi badal dega!

💀 2. Hidden Spikes & Traps

Game ka floor kabhi bhi bharosemand nahi hai. Khali dikhne wali zameen par kadam rakhte hi achanak invisible spikes bahar aa jayenge, platform gir jayega ya ceiling aapke upar achanak crush ho jayegi.

🔄 3. Instant Respawn

Level Devil ki tarah yahan maut ke baad koi loading ya delay nahi hoga. Jaise hi aap trap mein fas kar out honge, 0.1 second mein turant restart hoga taaki aap bina ruke dobara troll hone ke liye taiyar rahein!

🕹️ 4. Reverse Controls

Aapko lagta hai game control karna simple hai? Kuch levels mein Right dabane par Left jayega, Jump ka button zameen mein gira dega, aur gravity khud ulati ho jayegi. Aapka dimaag har pal test hoga.

🤬 5. Rage-Quit Warning

Strict Warning: Yeh game aapka blood pressure badha sakti hai! Screen par gussa nikalne ya phone fekne se bachein. Har level par jeette-jeette marna is game ka mukhya niyam hai.

😈 6. 100% Devil Traps

Har naye stage par naya dhokha milega. Fake doors, moving walls aur deceptive paths ke sath agar aapne sabhi levels paar kar liye, tabhi aap khud ko sachcha Z PAJAL Master keh sakte hain!
